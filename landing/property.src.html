The home

Sandstone, glass and half an acre of garden

Set back behind native grasses on a quiet lane, this is a house built around the light. Sandstone walls hold the warmth of the day, and full-height glass opens the living room straight onto the terrace.

Four bedrooms sit upstairs, away from the living areas, with a main suite that looks into the tree canopy. Downstairs, kitchen, dining and lounge run as one long room, anchored by a double-sided fireplace that takes care of Highlands winters.

Bowral's cafés and Corbett Plaza are four minutes by car. The station is five, for an easy run to the city.

  • North-facing living onto a covered stone terrace
  • Double-sided wood fireplace between lounge and dining
  • Main suite with walk-in robe and ensuite
  • Ducted heating and cooling throughout
  • Double garage with internal access and storage
